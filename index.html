HW-01 • ANALOG POWER STAGE Veroboard Build

Precision Linear Variable Power Supply (LM317 + BD139/BD140)

Working Principle: The LM317 maintains a constant 1.25V reference between OUT and ADJ. As load current increases across the series sense resistor, the BD139/BD140 complementary pass stage turns on to bypass high current safely around the IC. Output voltage follows Vout ≈ 1.25(1 + Rpot/R1).

LM317T BD139 / BD140 240Ω + 5kΩ Trimmer 1000µF + 100µF Caps
HW-02 • DISCRETE LOGIC Zero-IC Build

Discrete 3-Pin LDR Optical Sensor Module (BC547/BC548)

Working Principle: Incident light drops LDR resistance, raising base voltage on Q1 (BC547) above 0.7V. Q1 saturates, pulling Q2's base low and snapping Q2 off so OUT swings cleanly HIGH with crisp hysteresis and LED status indication on a compact 3-pin module.

BC547 / BC548 5mm LDR 10kΩ Trimmer VCC / GND / OUT
HW-03 • MIXED-SIGNAL BENCH Serial Plotter

Arduino UNO R3 Low-Freq Oscilloscope + NE555 Signal Generator

Working Principle: The NE555 generates configurable astable square/RC-ramp waveforms at f = 1.44 / ((Ra + 2Rb)C). A 10kΩ–10kΩ voltage divider protects the Arduino A0 10-bit ADC channel, streaming high-speed samples over 115200-baud UART to the Serial Plotter.

NE555 Timer Arduino UNO R3 10kΩ Protection Divider 115200 Baud Plotter
HW-04 • ELECTRO-FLUIDICS MCU-Free Relay Logic

Automated Touchless Water Dispenser (IR + 5V Relay + Pump)

Working Principle: Hand proximity reflects infrared energy into the photodiode receiver, driving the LM393 comparator output LOW. This activates the opto-isolated 5V relay coil, closing COM–NO contacts to energize the submersible pump while a reverse-biased 1N4007 flyback diode clamps inductive kickback.

IR Obstacle Module 5V Opto-Relay 1N4007 Flyback Diode 5V Submersible Pump
HW-05 • DIGITAL TELEMETRY 4-Bit Parallel LCD

Real-Time Environmental Monitor (Arduino UNO + DHT11 + JHD 162A)

Working Principle: The DHT11 transmits 40-bit calibrated temperature and relative humidity frames over a single-wire open-drain bus on D2 (with 10kΩ pull-up). The Arduino drives the JHD 162A HD44780 controller in 4-bit parallel mode while a 10kΩ trimmer adjusts V0 contrast.

DHT11 (Pin D2) JHD 162A 16x2 LCD 10kΩ Contrast Pot
